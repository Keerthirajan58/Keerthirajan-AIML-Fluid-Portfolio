## Brand assets

- LeetCode glyph and TCS mnemonic: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), `icons/leetcode.svg` and `icons/tcs.svg`, [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md). Brand trademarks remain with their owners. LeetCode is now the recognizable brand mark, replacing the previously requested generic journal-code symbol.
- SVCE emblem: [official college asset](https://www.svce.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/svce_logo.jpg), downloaded unmodified. Correct institution: Sri Venkateswara College of Engineering, Pennalur/Sriperumbudur, Tamil Nadu.
- Sparks Foundation emblem: unmodified logo reproduced in [an intern's article](https://medium.com/@brijnahalvadia3104/the-sparks-foundation-96c15a44fd41), [image source](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/1*lZiSsTxkN15NeTmMSjFa1A.png). Its old website redirected to an unrelated domain during verification; it is neither linked nor hotlinked here.
